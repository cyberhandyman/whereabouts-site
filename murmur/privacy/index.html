Privacy Policy

隐私政策

Effective date: 2026-08-13
生效日期：2026-08-13
Murmur collects no data. Period.
念念（Murmur）不收集任何数据。

What data do we collect?

None. Murmur has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no third-party SDKs. The app runs entirely on your device, and every feature works offline.

Where does your content live?

  • Scripts are stored only in your device's local storage and are never uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Speech recognition for voice tracking uses Apple's on-device recognition wherever supported. Transcripts are used transiently to align your position in the script and are discarded immediately — no audio and no transcripts are ever stored. On devices without on-device recognition, audio is processed by Apple's speech service under Apple's own privacy terms.
  • Recordings are saved only to your own system photo library.
  • Device remote traffic travels peer-to-peer between your two devices over the local network with encryption (Apple MultipeerConnectivity) — nothing passes through any server.
  • Preferences such as font size and speed are stored locally on your device.

Permissions

  • Microphone & Speech Recognition — only for voice-tracked scrolling (Smart Mode).
  • Camera — only for framing and recording in the record-while-prompting feature.
  • Photo Library (add-only) — only for saving videos you record.
  • Local Network — only for discovering and connecting the device remote.

All permissions are optional: they are requested only when you use the corresponding feature, and declining affects that feature alone.

Children's privacy

Murmur collects no data from anyone — including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date. No change will introduce data collection — "no data collected" is a product principle of Murmur.

Contact

Questions about privacy: pluginexpert2@gmail.com

我们收集什么数据？

什么都不收集。念念没有服务器、没有账号系统、没有统计分析、没有广告、没有任何第三方 SDK。App 完全在你的设备上运行，无需联网即可使用全部功能。

你的内容保存在哪里？

  • 提词稿：仅保存在你设备的本地存储中，不会上传到任何地方。卸载 App 会一并删除。
  • 语音识别：智能跟读优先使用 Apple 提供的设备端离线识别；识别结果只用于实时对齐台词位置，用完即弃，不保存录音、不保存文字记录。若设备不支持离线识别，音频将按照 Apple 的隐私条款由系统语音识别服务处理。
  • 录制的视频：只保存到你自己的系统相册。
  • 双机遥控：两台设备之间通过本地网络 / 点对点直连加密传输（Apple MultipeerConnectivity），数据不经过任何服务器。
  • 设置项：字号、速度等偏好保存在设备本地。

权限用途说明

  • 麦克风、语音识别：仅用于智能跟读（语音跟随滚动）。
  • 相机：仅用于提词拍摄功能的取景与录制。
  • 相册（仅添加）：仅用于保存你录制的视频。
  • 本地网络：仅用于双机遥控的设备发现与连接。

以上权限均为可选：不使用对应功能就不会触发授权请求，拒绝授权只影响对应功能本身。

儿童隐私

念念不收集任何人的数据，自然也不会收集儿童的数据。

政策变更

如果本政策发生变化，我们会在本页面更新并修改生效日期。变更不会引入数据收集——「不收集数据」是念念的产品原则。
