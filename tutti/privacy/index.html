Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-28
Tutti collects no data. Your scores stay on your Mac.

What data do we collect?

None. Tutti has no accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no third-party SDKs.

Your scores and settings

  • Scores are files you save where you choose (or in iCloud Drive if you choose so). Tutti never uploads them.
  • Settings, sound libraries and templates are stored locally in Tutti's own folder on your Mac.

Network use

  • When you ask for the MuseScore General sound library (about 200 MB), Tutti downloads it once from the public MuseScore mirror at the Oregon State University Open Source Lab (ftp.osuosl.org). Like any download, that server sees your IP address; Tutti sends nothing else.
  • Nothing else in Tutti uses the internet.

Permissions

  • Microphone — only to show the input level of the audio input device you select in Settings ▸ Audio. Nothing is recorded or saved.
  • Bluetooth — only to connect Bluetooth MIDI keyboards and wind controllers.
  • Files — Tutti opens and saves only the files and folders you pick.

Declining a permission affects only that feature.

Children's privacy

Tutti collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

Any change will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026-10-28
Tutti 打谱不收集任何数据，你的乐谱只在你的 Mac 上。

我们收集什么数据？

什么都不收集。Tutti 没有账号系统、没有自己的服务器、没有统计分析、没有广告、不做追踪，也不含任何第三方 SDK。

乐谱和设置

  • 乐谱是你自己保存在指定位置的文件（你选择 iCloud 云盘时由 iCloud 同步），Tutti 从不上传。
  • 设置、音色库和模板保存在本机 Tutti 自己的文件夹里。

联网

  • 当你选择下载 MuseScore General 音色库（约 200 MB）时，Tutti 会从俄勒冈州立大学开源实验室的 MuseScore 公共镜像（ftp.osuosl.org）下载一次。和任何下载一样，对方服务器能看到你的 IP 地址；除此之外 Tutti 不发送任何信息。
  • Tutti 的其他功能都不联网。

权限用途说明

  • 麦克风：只用于在「设置 ▸ 音频」里显示所选输入设备的电平，不录音、不保存。
  • 蓝牙：只用于连接蓝牙 MIDI 键盘和电吹管。
  • 文件：Tutti 只打开和保存你自己选择的文件和文件夹。

拒绝某项权限只影响对应功能。

儿童隐私

Tutti 不收集任何人的数据，自然也不会收集儿童的数据。

政策变更

如有变更，会在本页面更新并修改生效日期。
