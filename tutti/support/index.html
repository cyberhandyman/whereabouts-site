Tutti Support

Help, common questions and contact.
The complete manual is built into the app: Help ▸ Tutti Manual (⌘?). It is searchable and covers every feature.

Requirements

macOS 26 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel Macs. Interface and manual in English and Simplified Chinese (Settings ▸ General ▸ Interface language).

Common questions

Playback sounds thin, or there is no sound

Install the MuseScore General sound library from the Mixer (Play tab ▸ Mixer, ⌥⌘M; one download of about 200 MB). Until then Tutti plays with the General MIDI sounds built into macOS. Check the output device in Settings ▸ Audio.

My MIDI keyboard or wind controller does nothing

Connect it before or after opening Tutti and choose it in Settings ▸ MIDI. Bluetooth MIDI devices are connected with Connect Bluetooth MIDI Device… in the same pane. Wind controllers (EWI and similar) have their own input mode there.

Can I open Sibelius, MuseScore, Dorico or Finale files?

Export MusicXML from those programs (File ▸ Export ▸ MusicXML) and open the .musicxml / .mxl file in Tutti. MIDI files open too.

Can Tutti read a PDF or a photo of a score?

The App Store edition does not include music recognition. Recognize the score with the free Audiveris (audiveris.org), export MusicXML there, and open it in Tutti.

Transposing instruments

Tutti writes transposing instruments at written pitch and explains, for every instrument, what a written C sounds like. Switch to concert pitch on the View tab or with ⇧⌘T.

系统要求

macOS 26 或更新版本，Apple 芯片和 Intel 芯片的 Mac 都可以。界面和说明书有简体中文和英文（「设置 ▸ 通用 ▸ 界面语言」）。

常见问题

播放声音单薄，或者没有声音

在调音台里（「播放」选项卡 ▸ 调音台，⌥⌘M）安装 MuseScore General 音色库（下载一次，约 200 MB）。安装前 Tutti 用 macOS 自带的通用 MIDI 音色播放。另请在「设置 ▸ 音频」里检查输出设备。

MIDI 键盘或电吹管没反应

在打开 Tutti 之前或之后连接都可以，然后在「设置 ▸ MIDI」里选中它。蓝牙 MIDI 设备在这里点「连接蓝牙 MIDI 设备…」；电吹管（EWI 等）在这里有专门的输入模式。

能打开 Sibelius、MuseScore、Dorico、Finale 的文件吗？

请在这些软件里导出 MusicXML（文件 ▸ 导出 ▸ MusicXML），再用 Tutti 打开 .musicxml / .mxl 文件。MIDI 文件也能直接打开。

能识别 PDF 或乐谱照片吗？

App Store 版不含识谱功能。可以先用免费的 Audiveris（audiveris.org）识别并导出 MusicXML，再用 Tutti 打开。

移调乐器

Tutti 按记谱音高写移调乐器，并为每件乐器说明「谱面写 C，实际响的是什么音」。在「视图」选项卡或按 ⇧⌘T 可以切换实际音高。
