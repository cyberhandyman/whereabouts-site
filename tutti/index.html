Tutti

Music notation for Mac, in the spirit of Sibelius. Free.
Write scores the way you are used to: a ribbon, a six-layout keypad, an inspector and a piano keyboard. Engraving by Verovio, playback of everything you write.

Fast note input

Keypad with Sibelius' six layouts, computer keyboard, MIDI keyboards and wind controllers, step-time and Flexi-time recording.

1,164 markings

Dynamics, tempo words, articulations, ornaments, lines, repeats, noteheads, microtones and contemporary techniques, searchable by name.

Plays what you write

cresc., rit., pizz., a tempo, trills, tremolos and repeat structures all sound. MuseScore General sounds, Audio Unit instruments or MIDI out.

Engraving and layout

Make bars tighter or looser, make into system or page, lock format, cover and title pages, program and performance notes, parts.

627 instruments

The MuseScore instrument library with Chinese instruments added; transposing instruments explained in plain words.

Exchange

MusicXML and MIDI in and out; export PDF, PNG and audio (WAV, AIFF, M4A) for the score and every part.

macOS 26 or later · English and Simplified Chinese · no account, no ads, no data collected.

Tutti 打谱

Mac 上的打谱软件，Sibelius 的用法，免费。
按你熟悉的方式写谱：选项卡、六种布局的小键盘、属性面板和钢琴键盘。Verovio 排版，写什么就能演奏什么。

快速输入

Sibelius 六种布局的小键盘、电脑键盘、MIDI 键盘和电吹管，逐音输入与实时录入（Flexi-time）。

1164 种记号

力度、速度术语、演奏法、装饰音、线条、反复、符头、微分音、现代技法，用中文、英文、意大利文或拼音都能搜到。

写什么就演奏什么

cresc.、rit.、pizz.、a tempo、颤音、震音、反复跳转都会体现在回放里。MuseScore General 音色、Audio Unit 乐器或 MIDI 输出。

排版与版面

逐小节排紧排松、合成一行或一页、锁定版面，封面、扉页、作品介绍与演出说明页，分谱。

627 种乐器

MuseScore 乐器库，全部有中文名，另含常用民族乐器；移调乐器用大白话讲清楚。

交换与导出

MusicXML、MIDI 导入导出；总谱和每份分谱都能导出 PDF、PNG 和音频（WAV、AIFF、M4A）。

需要 macOS 26 或更新版本 · 简体中文和英文界面 · 无需账号，无广告，不收集任何数据。
