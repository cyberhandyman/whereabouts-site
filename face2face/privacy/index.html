Privacy Policy

隐私政策

Effective date: 2026-10-18
生效日期：2026-10-18
Face2Face Live Translator collects no data. Everything happens on your device.
面面翻译器（Face2Face Live Translator）不收集任何数据，所有处理都在你的设备上完成。

What data do we collect?

None. Face2Face has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no third-party SDKs. Nothing you say, type or read is sent anywhere.

How your conversations are processed

  • Speech recognition runs on your device with Apple's on-device speech models. The app never uses a server for recognition: if a language can't be recognized on your device, the app tells you instead. Audio is never recorded or saved.
  • Translation runs on your device with Apple's Translation framework. Translation models are downloaded from Apple by the system, the same way Apple's Translate app does, and are shared with it.
  • Reading aloud uses the voices built into iOS, on your device.
  • Conversation history (if you keep it on) is saved only in the app's storage on your device, is not included in iCloud backups, and can be deleted at any time or automatically after 7 or 30 days. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • Settings are stored locally on your device.

Permissions

  • Microphone & Speech Recognition — to hear both people and turn speech into text, on the device.
  • Camera (iPhone Duo only) — iOS lets apps show content on the iPhone Duo outer display only while the app's camera is on. Face2Face turns the camera on only while it uses the outer display. Camera frames are discarded immediately: they are never shown, recorded, saved or analyzed.

Permissions are requested only when needed. Declining affects only that feature.

Children's privacy

Face2Face collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

Any change will be posted on this page with a new effective date. "No data collected" is a product principle and will not change.

Contact

Questions about privacy: pluginexpert2@gmail.com

我们收集什么数据？

什么都不收集。面面翻译器没有服务器、没有账号系统、没有统计分析、没有广告、没有任何第三方 SDK。你说的、打的、看到的内容都不会发送到任何地方。

对话是怎么处理的？

  • 语音识别：使用 Apple 提供的设备端语音模型，在本机完成。App 从不使用服务器识别：某种语言在你的设备上无法识别时，App 会直接告诉你。不录音、不保存音频。
  • 翻译：使用 Apple 的 Translation 框架在本机完成。翻译模型由系统从 Apple 下载，与系统“翻译”App 相同并共用。
  • 朗读：使用 iOS 内置的语音，在本机完成。
  • 对话记录（如果你选择保存）只存放在本机的 App 存储中，不包含在 iCloud 备份里，可以随时删除，也可以设置 7 天或 30 天后自动删除。卸载 App 会一并删除。
  • 设置项：保存在设备本地。

权限用途说明

  • 麦克风、语音识别：听见双方说话，并在本机把语音转成文字。
  • 相机（仅 iPhone Duo）：iOS 只允许 App 在相机开启时使用 iPhone Duo 外屏。面面翻译器只在使用外屏期间打开相机，相机画面会被立即丢弃，不会显示、录制、保存或分析。

权限只在需要时请求，拒绝只影响对应功能。

儿童隐私

面面翻译器不收集任何人的数据，自然也不会收集儿童的数据。

政策变更

如有变更，会在本页面更新并修改生效日期。“不收集数据”是面面翻译器的产品原则，不会改变。
