Getting started

  • Put the phone between you. The top half is turned to face the other person.
  • Tap a language name to choose it — each person picks their own.
  • Whoever is talking taps the microphone on their side; tap it again to stop. Once it's on, tap Auto and the phone switches sides by itself when someone pauses.
  • Tap any sentence to hear it read aloud. Tap your own sentence to fix a word the phone misheard.

Using it offline

Open Settings → Offline Languages and download both languages before you travel. After that, everything works in Airplane Mode. Translation models are shared with Apple's Translate app.

iPhone Duo

Open the phone: you read the inner display and the other person reads the outer display, in their language — both of you can tap. iOS lets apps use the outer display only while the camera is on, so Face2Face turns the camera on just for that; nothing is shown, recorded or saved. If you prefer not to, turn off Settings → Use the Outer Display and the inner display shows both sides. Closed, the app works on the outer display like on any phone.

Big captions

Tap the captions button for large, high-contrast captions in one language — for someone hard of hearing, a noisy room or a mask. Pinch to resize, hold to pause, swipe up to clear, and tap the keyboard to type something to be read aloud.

Languages

Arabic, Chinese (Mandarin and Taiwan Mandarin), Cantonese, Dutch, English, French, German, Hindi, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, Thai, Turkish, Ukrainian and Vietnamese, using Apple's on-device models. Cantonese is recognized natively and translated as Traditional Chinese, so some colloquial words may come out less accurately.

It doesn't hear me

  • Check Settings → Face2Face: Microphone and Speech Recognition must be on.
  • Keep your fingers off the microphones and speak towards the phone.
  • If a language shows "Not available on this phone", your device has no on-device model for it — you can still type.

开始使用

  • 把手机放在你们中间，上半屏会转向对方。
  • 点语言名字选择语言，两个人各选各的。
  • 谁说话，谁点自己那一边的麦克风；再点一下结束。麦克风打开后点自动，有人停顿时手机会自动换到另一边。
  • 点任意一句可以朗读；点自己说的那句可以改掉听错的字。

离线使用

出发前打开 设置 → 离线语言，把两种语言都下载好。之后开飞行模式也能用。翻译模型与系统“翻译”App 共用。

iPhone Duo

展开手机：你看内屏，对方看外屏上自己的语言，两边都能点。iOS 只允许 App 在相机开启时使用外屏，所以面面翻译器只为此打开相机，不显示、不录制、不保存任何画面。不想用的话，在 设置 → 使用外屏 关掉，内屏会同时显示两边。合上时，App 在外屏上像普通手机一样使用。

大字字幕

点字幕按钮，就能用一种语言显示高对比度的大字字幕，适合听力不好的长辈、嘈杂的环境或戴口罩的时候。双指缩放字号，按住暂停，上滑清屏，点键盘打字让手机读出来。

支持的语言

阿拉伯语、中文（普通话、台湾国语）、粤语、荷兰语、英语、法语、德语、印地语、印尼语、意大利语、日语、韩语、波兰语、葡萄牙语、俄语、西班牙语、泰语、土耳其语、乌克兰语、越南语，均使用 Apple 的设备端模型。粤语可以原生识别，翻译时按繁体中文处理，部分口语字可能翻得不够准确。

听不到我说话？

  • 检查 设置 → 面面翻译器：麦克风和语音识别都要打开。
  • 手指别挡住麦克风，朝手机说话。
  • 如果某种语言显示“这部手机暂不支持”，说明设备上没有这种语言的本机模型，仍然可以打字。
